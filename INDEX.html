Welcome to Oo’yiit

Oo'yiit (eat) is one of the best Indigenous-owned restaurants located in the heart of Mohkínstsis (Calgary). We locally source our ingredients; farm to table. Our belief is in low product waste; nose to tail. Here at Oo'yiit, we encourage Indigenous employment but are open to all. Our goal is to help bring nutrition from traditional foods and the skills to make them back into our Indigenous communities. Join us to dine in, order to-go, or order one of our meal kits. A portion of our sales from selected items will help bring affordable nutritional items back to Indigenous communities, on and off- reserve.
