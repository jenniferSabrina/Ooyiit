About

Jennifer Fraser, the owner of Oo’yiit (eat), is an Indigenous business owner, born and raised in Mohkínstsis (Calgary), whose priority is to honour the traditional territory of the Niitsítapi (Blackfoot) in Treaty 7. Jennifer has been working in the restaurant industry for 15+ years in the front of the house. While working in restaurants, Jennifer has paid close attention to the chefs and learned from some of the best, well-known local chefs. She took her passion for food and beverage and shared it with the community for 10 years with her food truck.

Here at Oo’yiit, we serve an Indigenous-inspired menu through storytelling. We are always honouring the teaching of using every part of the buffalo, so we minimize our waste, aiming to use every part of the animal. The skills we use and teach are butchering and in-house made recipes. Our goal is to source our ingredients locally, but we are open to sourcing from throughout North America. This is an example of how using our traditional ways of trading and bartering can work well in the modern world.

We strive for excellence in our service from the moment the food leaves our kitchen or drinks leave the bar and arrive at the table. We want your experience to be top-notch and showcase the best of Indigenous culture.

Oo’yiit (eat) offers meal kits that can be ordered online and delivered to your home. With easy-to-follow recipes, you can have high-quality ingredients and follow along with us in this traditional culinary journey.
