Giving Back

There are plenty of ways you can help Indigenous and non-Indigenous peoples while dining with Oo’yiit (eat).

  • You can buy meal kits for a family and choose to send them to a specific family, whether in the city or on a reservation.
  • You can choose to donate money to our charity to help fund an Indigenous student to study culinary arts or hospitality.
  • Have a skill you wish to share? This is the perfect opportunity to teach our staff.
  • Hosting a seniors' luncheon where you can donate to the charity. We will provide a preset menu and arrange all the planning.
  • Going out to eat at a restaurant is a luxury not everybody has access to. You can donate to the charity and host a family meal.
Disclaimer: If you would like to leave the name of the person/family/business who donated, please feel free. If not, you can choose to remain anonymous.
